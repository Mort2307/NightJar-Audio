## 2. Age, identity and voluntary participation

2.1 The Performer confirms that they are at least 18 years old on the date of signing and on every date on which the performance is recorded.

2.2 Before recording or publication, the Operator must complete its required adult-status and identity checks for every real person whose performance appears in the work. 2.3 The Performer confirms that they are choosing to participate voluntarily, have had a reasonable opportunity to read the script or performance brief, understand the intended use and have been able to ask questions or seek independent advice.

2.4 The Performer must not be pressured to perform material outside the agreed script, brief or boundaries. They may stop a recording session and raise a concern. The Operator must not punish a Performer for refusing an unagreed act or content change. The parties will then discuss whether to revise the brief, reschedule or end the engagement and what payment is due for work already completed.

## 3. Consent to recording and agreed use

3.1 The Performer consents to the Operator and the named recording producer making and editing a recording of the specific performance described in section 1, including the reasonable technical processes required to capture, edit, mix, master, store and prepare that recording for the uses selected below.

3.2 The Performer authorises the following uses of the identified performance (tick only what is agreed):
- [ ] Private review and quality assurance by authorised NightJar personnel.
- [ ] Publication and making available to authenticated NightJar members.
- [ ] Subscription-funded streaming or access through NightJar.
- [ ] Short previews, trailers or extracts that relate directly to the identified release.
- [ ] Distribution to named hosting, streaming or delivery suppliers acting for NightJar.
- [ ] Use in NightJar's own marketing outside the platform (must specify formats and duration): [INSERT / NOT GRANTED].
- [ ] Other specific use: [INSERT].

3.3 No unchecked use is authorised. Permission is limited to the identified work, agreed purpose, media, territory and period stated in this Schedule and any selected rights option below.

3.4 The Operator may make ordinary technical edits for timing, clarity, noise reduction, mixing, mastering, formatting, accessibility and removal of mistakes. It must not materially change the meaning of the performance, create a misleading portrayal, add words or conduct outside the agreed performance, or use edits to place the Performer in an unconsented context.

3.5 The Operator must not use the performance to create a synthetic voice, voice clone, imitation, digital replica or new performance. It must not create or commission an artificial or digitally altered intimate depiction of the Performer. It must not use the performance to train, fine-tune or improve generative AI or speech models. Any materially different synthetic or intimate use would require a separate, specific, informed written agreement and must be lawful; this Schedule does not provide that permission. Optional speech-to-text transcription requires a separate Schedule G and a verified provider that contractually respects the no-training restriction.

## 7. Review, withdrawal and complaint route

7.1 Before publication, the Performer may request a reasonable opportunity to review the final edit solely to check that it remains within the agreed scope and boundaries. The review period is [INSERT] calendar days. If no review is requested or response is received in that period, the Operator may proceed only if the agreed brief and rights permissions are otherwise complete.

7.2 The Performer may report an edit, use or publication that they reasonably believe exceeds this Schedule through [INSERT SUPPORT CONTACT]. The Operator must acknowledge the report within 5 Working Days and review it promptly. It may temporarily restrict access while a credible safety, consent or rights issue is investigated.

7.3 Before publication, the Performer may request that the performance not be released. The parties must discuss the request in good faith, taking account of work already performed, payments due, third-party commitments and the agreed rights option. Nothing requires a Performer to continue a recording session or perform material outside the agreed boundaries.

7.4 After publication, withdrawal of a consent or licence is governed by the rights option and any mandatory legal rights. The Operator must not promise unconditional deletion where lawful subscriber access, legal obligations or a valid assignment/licence prevents it; equally, it must not claim a wider or longer permission than the Performer signed.

7.5 Nothing in this Schedule prevents a Performer from reporting abuse, coercion, unlawful conduct, a safety concern or a rights infringement, or from obtaining legal advice.

## 8. Data and confidentiality

The Operator will handle the recording, contact details, verification status and related personal data in accordance with its Privacy Notice and applicable data-protection law. Access to identity verification evidence must be restricted to authorised personnel and handled through the designated verification provider. A voice recording is not automatically special-category biometric data merely because it contains a voice; any biometric identification processing requires a separate legal assessment and appropriate safeguards.

The Performer must keep unpublished scripts, recordings and confidential project information secure and must not disclose them except as authorised or legally permitted.

The Operator confirms that it will use the performance only within the scope granted by this Schedule and the applicable project agreement.
