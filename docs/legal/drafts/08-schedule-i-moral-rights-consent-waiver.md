## 3. Select one option

### Option A — Limited consent

- [ ] The Author consents to the specific act described in section 2. This consent applies only to that act and does not waive any other moral right or authorise an unrelated use.

### Option B — Limited waiver

- [ ] To the extent legally permitted, the Author waives only the moral right(s) expressly ticked in section 2 in relation to the identified work and the specific uses described there. The waiver is made in favour of the Operator and, only to the extent necessary for those uses, its authorised licensees and successors in title.

The waiver does not:
(a) transfer copyright ownership;
(b) authorise a use outside the agreed scope;
(c) waive rights in unrelated or future works;
(d) permit false attribution or imply the Author created work they did not create; or
(e) waive any right that cannot legally be waived.

### Option C — No waiver

- [ ] No waiver or additional consent is given. The Author retains the relevant moral rights in full. The Operator must adapt the planned use so that it respects those rights.
