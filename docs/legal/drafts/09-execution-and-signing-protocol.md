# NIGHTJAR — CONTRACT EXECUTION AND ADMINISTRATION PROTOCOL

**Internal operating procedure — not a contract**

## 1. Contract assembly

Before asking anyone to sign, create one contributor pack containing:
1. the current Master Contributor Agreement;
2. only the schedules that apply to that person's actual work;
3. a Statement of Work for every paid commission;
4. a separate Performer Release (Schedule H) for every identifiable performer;
5. a specific Moral Rights Consent/Waiver (Schedule I) only where a real issue has been identified;
6. Schedule E for each work separately designated a NightJar Original; and
7. any data-processing terms only if the contributor actually processes personal data for the Operator.

The creator-to-creator collaboration form (Schedule F) is a standalone agreement between those creators. It is not part of the Master Contributor Agreement and NightJar must not sign it as a party unless NightJar is genuinely taking on obligations in a separately drafted agreement.

## 2. Pre-signature checks

The contract administrator must confirm:
- the Operator's exact legal name, legal form and service address;
- the Contributor's full legal name and notice email;
- the authority of the person signing for the Operator;
- the Contributor's approved role and the actual work being agreed;
- all selected schedules and their current versions;
- the named work, script/brief/version and deliverables;
- the fee, payment dates, VAT wording and expenses, where relevant;
- the rights option (retained ownership, limited licence, exclusive licence or assignment);
- all authors, sound-recording owners, performers and third-party rights holders;
- any required Schedule H performer releases and Schedule I moral-rights consents/waivers;
- the exact content boundaries, credit/anonymity choice and permitted promotional uses;
- age and identity checks through the secure verification workflow where required;
- any transcription provider, purpose, retention and no-training evidence before Schedule G is accepted; and
- the Creator Pool eligibility table and percentage applicable to the accounting period, where Schedule C applies.

Do not leave a material field blank and expect a later platform setting or chat message to complete it. Resolve the terms in a signed variation.

## 3. Electronic signature and evidence

Electronic signatures can be used for many English-law contracts and can satisfy a statutory signature requirement where the signatory intends to authenticate the document and the relevant execution formalities are met. The Law Commission confirms this general position. This does not mean a generic registration checkbox is sufficient for every document.

For each signed agreement or rights instrument, store:
- the signer's full name and role;
- the exact document title, version and schedule identifiers;
- a preserved copy of the exact document as signed;
- a cryptographic hash or other reliable integrity record for that copy;
- the signature or signing action showing intention to authenticate;
- the date and time (with timezone);
- the authentication and delivery evidence provided by the signing method; and
- a record showing that the completed copy was made available to each party.

A click-through acceptance of ordinary platform terms must be kept separate from signature of a copyright assignment, performer-rights assignment, exclusive licence or moral-rights waiver. Use a proper e-signature flow for rights transfers. Do not rely on a checkbox unless the flow is specifically designed and validated to capture the signatory's intention and the signed document.

## 4. Sequence of events

1. Agree the brief, rights, fee, boundaries and dates.
2. Complete the legal and operational checks.
3. Provide the complete draft pack and allow the person time to read and ask questions.
4. Obtain signatures from every required party and rights holder.
5. Provide each party with the final signed documents.
6. Store the records against the relevant contributor and unique work/release reference.
7. Only then accept delivery, start recording or release the work in accordance with the agreement.
8. Pay on the agreed dates and retain a clear payment record.

For urgent changes, pause recording, publication or exploitation until the relevant written variation or additional release is completed.

## 5. Version control and amendments

- Every document must have a version number and effective date.
- Never silently replace a document a creator has already signed.
- A material change must be explained and accepted before it applies to that creator.
- Keep previous versions and their acceptance evidence.
- Keep a per-project rights ledger identifying the work, rights holder, rights granted, term, territory, media, restrictions, signed instrument and payment status.
- No creator's rights may be expanded retrospectively through a website policy update.

## 6. Record retention and access

Store signed contracts in a restricted-access legal records area. Access should be limited to authorised administrators and people who need it for legal, finance or operational reasons. Do not store raw identity documents in the contracts folder unless a specific legal requirement has been assessed; retain verification status and necessary references through the designated secure verification system.

Apply a documented retention schedule. Keep records for as long as reasonably required for contract administration, rights evidence, accounting, disputes or legal obligations, then securely delete or anonymise them when no longer required. Place records on a documented legal hold only where there is a genuine need.

## 7. Separate compliance workstreams

This contract pack does not replace:
- the public NightJar Terms of Use;
- the listener subscription terms and cancellation process;
- the Privacy Notice and data protection complaints process;
- age-assurance and performer safeguarding processes;
- internal moderation, rights complaint and takedown procedures;
- tax, VAT, payroll or employment-status assessments; or
- insurance and financial recordkeeping.

The UK subscription contract reforms under the Digital Markets, Competition and Consumers Act 2024 and the draft Consumer Subscription Contracts Regulations published in October 2026 require a separate launch review. As at 8 November 2026, the draft Regulations on legislation.gov.uk are still marked as draft legislation, with a proposed commencement date of 1 January 2027. Recheck the made Statutory Instrument and commencement position before the launch date.

## 8. Approval status

No draft in this folder is approved for signature until:
- Meg and Mike have approved the content;
- all mandatory fields and policy decisions are complete;
- the legal operator has been confirmed;
- the final versions have been checked against the live NightJar workflows; and
- any issue identified as requiring a solicitor or accountant has been referred and resolved.

The contract administrator must not mark a pack “executed” until all required signatures and records are present.
