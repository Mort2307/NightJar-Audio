# NightJar Creator Contract Framework

**DRAFT FOR INTERNAL REVIEW ONLY — NOT FOR SIGNATURE OR ISSUE**

Version 0.2 | Proposed law: England and Wales | 8 November 2026

## Recommended structure

Retain one Master Contributor Agreement as the common umbrella contract. Attach only the schedules that apply to each contributor. Every paid commission also needs its own signed project Statement of Work. Keep separate rights documents for each performer and for any moral-rights consent/waiver. This avoids conflicting standalone contracts and stops a contributor accidentally accepting terms that do not apply.

## Contract pack

1. **01 — Master Contributor Agreement:** common rules, relationship, approvals, rights framework, safety, payment principles, disputes, termination, liability and notices.
2. **02 — Schedules A–C:** Voluntary Contributions; Paid Commissioned Work and Statement of Work; Creator Pool and Revenue Share.
3. **03 — Schedules D–G:** Confidentiality and conditional data-processing addendum; NightJar Originals; standalone direct creator-to-creator collaboration template; optional speech-to-text permission.
4. **07 — Schedule H:** Performer Consent, Recording and Use Release. A separate form must be signed by every performer for each recording/work.
5. **08 — Schedule I:** Moral Rights Consent or Waiver. Use only where a specific moral-rights issue requires a separate written instrument.
6. **05 — Role-specific scope templates:** VA Creator, Writer, Audio Editor, Artist and combined-role engagements. These support, but do not replace, the Statement of Work.
7. **06 — Plain-English glossary:** explanations of legal and commercial terms.
8. **09 — Execution and Signing Protocol:** document assembly, identity checks, e-signature evidence, recordkeeping and sequence.
9. **04 — Review Register:** unresolved legal, factual and commercial decisions.
10. **10 — Internal Contract Audit Report:** clause-by-clause assessment, legal-risk summary and items that must be resolved before signature.

## Important distinction

Schedule F is a standalone contract between creators themselves. It is not incorporated into the Master Contributor Agreement and NightJar is not a party to it.

Schedules H and I are ancillary, work-specific instruments. The relevant performer or author/rightsholder signs them directly. A signature on the Master Agreement does not substitute for these separate rights instruments.

## Completion and approval

The legal operator must be correctly identified before signature. NightJar Audio is a brand/trading name unless confirmed otherwise; do not describe it as a limited company unless incorporation has taken place. Complete every project-specific field, select applicable schedules, attach a Statement of Work for each commission, and store the final signed records.

The draft reflects the current operating principles: voluntary work remains creator-owned under a limited platform licence; paid commissioned work is assigned only under a signed work-specific instrument and after full payment; the proposed Creator Pool is 50% of Eligible Net Subscription Revenue, shared equally per eligible person; Meg opts out; creator-to-creator payments remain directly between creators; NightJar Originals require separate opt-in; transcription permission is narrow and excludes model training/voice cloning; direct bank transfers from Monzo are the current payment route.

These are internal drafts, not legal certification. They must not be uploaded as live terms, accepted in-app or sent to creators until Meg and Mike have reviewed them, mandatory details and commercial policies are complete, and required legal/accounting advice has been obtained.
