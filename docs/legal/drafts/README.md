# NightJar Creator Contract Framework

**DRAFT FOR INTERNAL REVIEW ONLY — NOT FOR SIGNATURE OR ISSUE**

Version 0.1 | Proposed law: England and Wales | 8 November 2026

## Recommended structure

Retain one Master Contributor Agreement, but make it the umbrella agreement rather than a document that tries to contain every possible arrangement. Attach only the schedules that apply to each contributor. Each paid project also needs its own signed Statement of Work. This avoids contradictory standalone contracts and prevents a creator from accidentally accepting terms that do not apply to them.

## Contract pack

1. Master Contributor Agreement — common rules, relationship, approvals, rights framework, safety, payment principles, disputes and termination.
2. Schedule A — Voluntary Contributions: creator retains ownership; NightJar receives a limited, non-exclusive platform licence.
3. Schedule B — Paid Commissioned Work: project scope, fee, delivery, revisions and signed copyright assignment after full payment.
4. Schedule C — Creator Pool: proposed 50% allocation, equal individual shares, eligibility, statements and direct bank payments.
5. Schedule D — Confidentiality and Data Handling, including a processor addendum only where the contributor actually processes personal data for NightJar.
6. Schedule E — NightJar Originals: separate, express opt-in exclusivity or assignment for a named work.
7. Schedule F — Direct Creator Collaboration: agreement between creators themselves; NightJar is not a party or payment intermediary.
8. Schedule G — Speech-to-Text Permission: narrow transcription permission, no model training or voice cloning.
9. Role-specific scope templates — VA Creator, Writer, Audio Editor, Artist and combined-role projects.
10. Plain-English glossary — definitions of legal and commercial terms.
11. Review Register — open legal, operational and commercial decisions.

## Completion and approval

The legal operator must be correctly identified before signature. NightJar Audio is a brand/trading name unless confirmed otherwise; do not describe it as a limited company unless incorporation has taken place. Complete every placeholder, select applicable schedules, attach a Statement of Work for each commission, and store signed versions.

These are internal drafts, not legal certification. They must not be uploaded as live terms, accepted in-app or sent to creators until Meg and Mike have reviewed them and the outstanding points in the Review Register are resolved.
