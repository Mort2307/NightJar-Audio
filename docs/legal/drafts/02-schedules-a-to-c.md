# Schedule A — Voluntary Contributions

## A1. Nature of voluntary work

A Voluntary Contribution is work the Contributor chooses to submit without a separate project commission fee. It is not paid commissioned work merely because NightJar reviews, edits, credits, publishes or monetises it. Any separate payment must be recorded in a signed Statement of Work or written variation.

## A2. Ownership

The Contributor retains ownership of copyright and other intellectual-property rights they own in the Voluntary Contribution. Where work is created jointly, ownership remains with the actual rights holders under applicable law and any separate agreement. A Contributor must not grant rights on behalf of another person without authority.

## A3. Licence to NightJar

When the Contributor submits a work for consideration, they grant the Operator a temporary, non-exclusive licence to receive, store, review and moderate that submission solely to decide whether to accept it. If NightJar rejects the work or the Contributor withdraws it before acceptance, NightJar must not publish or otherwise exploit it and must securely delete ordinary working copies within 30 calendar days, except for a restricted copy reasonably required for a legal hold, dispute evidence or secure backup.

If NightJar accepts the work for hosting or publication, the Contributor grants the Operator a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence while the work is hosted to:
(a) receive, store, back up and technically process the work;
(b) review, moderate, format, encode, transcode, quality-check and secure it;
(c) reproduce, communicate, display and make it available to NightJar members through the platform in the publication format selected by the Contributor and accepted by NightJar;
(d) make proportionate technical edits, create accessibility versions, previews, thumbnails, trailers and promotional extracts reasonably connected with the release; and
(e) use the Contributor's approved professional name and agreed credit to identify and promote that release.

The licence does not permit NightJar to sell or assign the Contributor's copyright, sublicense the work for unrelated commercial exploitation, publish private source files, create a derivative work unrelated to the agreed release, train generative AI models on the work or clone the Contributor's voice.

Hosting, streaming, storage, security, moderation, accessibility and delivery suppliers may process the work only as needed to provide services to NightJar and must be subject to appropriate confidentiality and data-protection safeguards.

The licence is non-exclusive: the Contributor may use and license their own work elsewhere, provided they do not disclose confidential material, breach third-party rights or breach a separately signed exclusivity agreement.

## A4. Removal requests and end of licence

The Contributor may request removal at any time through [INSERT SUPPORT ADDRESS] or the designated NightJar workflow. NightJar will acknowledge the request within 5 Working Days and will normally remove public access within 14 calendar days after confirming the request and identifying the work.

If the work is subject to active paid listener access, a legal hold, credible rights dispute, safety investigation or technical backup cycle, NightJar may need a reasonable additional period or may restrict access while the issue is resolved. It must explain the reason and keep any delay proportionate. The parties must not use this clause to create indefinite public availability after a valid removal request.

On completion of removal, the licence to make the work publicly available to members ends, except for a limited wind-down expressly agreed in writing or strictly necessary to complete already-paid listener access in a fair and lawful way. NightJar may retain a restricted archival copy only where reasonably necessary for legal compliance, dispute evidence, security, financial records or disaster recovery. It must not continue ordinary public exploitation of that copy. Backup copies must be overwritten or deleted in the ordinary secure backup cycle.

Removal does not cancel a fee already earned or an accrued Creator Pool entitlement. It also does not revoke an assignment or licence granted under a separate signed Commission or NightJar Original agreement.

## A5. Rights clearance and promotion

Before submission, the Contributor must identify third-party material and obtain permissions needed for the uses in A3. This includes samples, stock assets, music, fonts, external performers, co-writers, artwork, adaptations and material with separate licence restrictions. NightJar may request reasonable evidence of permission.

NightJar may use the Contributor's agreed professional name, approved profile image and credit solely to identify, promote and direct members to the relevant release or profile. Wider endorsement, unrelated paid advertising, merchandise use or use of the Contributor's image or voice requires separate permission.

## A6. No automatic exclusivity or AI permission

This Schedule is non-exclusive. A Voluntary Contribution does not become a NightJar Original unless Schedule E is separately signed. Uploading content does not give permission for generative AI training, model improvement, synthetic voice creation, voice cloning or unrelated AI processing. Optional transcription requires Schedule G and verification of the provider and its settings.

# Schedule B — Paid Commissioned Work

## B1. Commission process

No commission begins until both parties have signed or reliably accepted a Statement of Work identifying the work, fee, delivery requirements, deadlines, revision allowance and rights arrangement. A profile approval, message or upload is not a commission.

The Statement of Work must state whether the fee is inclusive or exclusive of VAT where VAT is properly chargeable, and whether expenses are included or separately reimbursed. The Contributor is not required to begin until the Statement of Work is complete and any agreed advance has been paid.

## B2. Delivery and acceptance

The Contributor must deliver the agreed materials in the specified format by the agreed deadline, subject to agreed extensions. The Operator must review delivery within 10 Working Days unless another reasonable period is stated.

If delivery materially fails to meet the written specification, the Operator must provide a clear list of issues. The Contributor will have a reasonable opportunity to correct them within the agreed revision allowance. Revisions cover only the agreed scope. New creative direction, additional episodes, substantial rewrites, new formats or extra recording sessions require a written variation and, where appropriate, an additional fee.

The Operator must not unreasonably delay acceptance or use minor issues to avoid payment for work that substantially meets the specification. If the Operator does not respond within the review period, the parties must agree an extension or the Operator must explain what remains to be reviewed; silence alone does not automatically waive serious defects or create acceptance unless the Statement of Work expressly says so.

## B3. Fee and payment

The fee and milestones are those in the Statement of Work. Unless it states otherwise, the Operator will pay the final undisputed balance within 14 calendar days after acceptance and receipt of a valid invoice or other legally sufficient payment request.

If part of an invoice is disputed in good faith, the Operator must explain the disputed amount promptly and pay any undisputed amount by the due date. Payment is by direct bank transfer to the Contributor's nominated account, currently using the Operator's nominated Monzo account. NightJar does not route commission payments between creators.

The Contributor must disclose VAT registration and provide a valid VAT invoice where required. No party should charge VAT unless legally entitled or required.

## B4. Copyright and other rights assignment

**This clause applies only where assignment is expressly selected in the Statement of Work.**

In consideration of the agreed fee, the Contributor assigns to the Operator all copyright and other transferable intellectual-property rights the Contributor owns or is entitled to assign in the specifically identified Commissioned Work. The assignment is worldwide, for the full period of the relevant rights, including renewals and extensions, and includes the rights to reproduce, publish, distribute, communicate, make available, adapt, edit, translate, combine, market, license and otherwise exploit the work in all media and formats, to the extent those rights exist and are legally transferable.

For copyright that comes into existence after this Agreement is signed as part of the specifically identified Commissioned Work, the Contributor agrees to assign that future copyright to the Operator by way of a present assignment of future copyright, so that it vests in the Operator when it comes into existence, to the extent permitted by law. This clause is limited to the commissioned work described in the Statement of Work; it does not cover unrelated future work.

Where the Contributor is also a Performer and owns performer property rights in the recorded performance, those rights are assigned only if the Statement of Work expressly selects that transfer and the Contributor signs Schedule H identifying the performance and rights. The assignment must not be inferred from the assignment of copyright in a script or sound recording.

The assignment takes effect only when the Contributor has received the full agreed fee for the relevant work. Until then, the Contributor grants the Operator a limited, non-exclusive licence to review, test and internally assess the delivery, but not to release or commercially exploit it.

The assignment does not transfer third-party material, pre-existing tools/templates/methods/know-how identified in the Statement of Work, rights that cannot legally be assigned, or rights expressly reserved. Any listed pre-existing material incorporated into the work is licensed to the Operator on a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free basis only as incorporated in or reasonably necessary to use and exploit the commissioned work. Wider use requires separate agreement.

The Contributor must obtain written assignments or licences from co-authors, producers, performers, editors, artists or other rights holders whose contribution is intended to transfer. The Contributor cannot assign rights they do not own or control. The Contributor confirms, to the best of their knowledge after reasonable checks, that they have not granted conflicting rights in the commissioned work and will not grant such rights before the agreed transfer takes effect.

## B5. Moral rights and performer rights

Moral rights are not assigned with copyright. Any waiver or consent must be a separate, specific written instrument signed by the relevant author and limited to the uses genuinely required for the project. A contributor must not be required to waive more than is reasonably necessary. Where the Operator only needs to make routine edits, formatting or adaptations, a limited consent for those identified uses may be preferable to a general waiver.

A Performer must provide written consent to the recording/fixation and agreed uses of their performance. If the parties intend an assignment of the Performer's property rights, the performer must sign the specific assignment in Schedule H. A release of a script or recording does not automatically clear the rights in the performance.

## B6. Licence alternative

The parties may select a licence instead of assignment. The Statement of Work must specify exclusivity, territory, duration, media, permitted adaptations, sublicensing, credit and any continuing payment. If neither a complete assignment nor licence is selected, the Contributor retains ownership and the Operator may only evaluate the work under the limited review licence in B4.

## B7. Cancellation

If the Operator cancels before work begins, it must pay approved non-refundable third-party costs. If it cancels after work begins, it must pay for work completed up to cancellation and approved unavoidable commitments, subject to the Statement of Work. If the Contributor abandons work without lawful excuse, the Operator may withhold payment for undelivered work but must pay for a completed portion it elects to keep or use. Rights in unfinished or unpaid work remain with the Contributor unless a separate written settlement states otherwise.

# Schedule C — Creator Pool and Revenue Share

## C1. Nature of the Creator Pool

The Creator Pool is a contractual revenue-sharing arrangement for eligible approved creators, separate from commission fees. The proposed starting allocation is **50% of Eligible Net Subscription Revenue**. The remaining allocation to NightJar operating funds, owner distributions and any optional tax reserve is governed by a separate finance policy and does not reduce the Creator Pool allocation unless changed prospectively under C10.

A Creator Pool payment is not a wage, salary, guaranteed minimum payment, commission for a particular item or ownership interest in NightJar. This description does not determine employment or tax status.

## C2. Eligible Net Subscription Revenue

Eligible Net Subscription Revenue means subscription money actually received and settled by the Operator for the relevant period, less only:
(a) VAT properly payable on those receipts;
(b) refunds actually paid or credited;
(c) chargebacks and payment reversals actually incurred; and
(d) third-party payment processing fees directly charged on those receipts and evidenced in settlement records.

A Monzo bank transfer is not treated as carrying a card-processing fee. A bank fee may be deducted only if actually charged, directly attributable to the relevant receipt or payout and transparently recorded. If no third-party processing fee is charged, no fee deduction may be made.

The Operator must not deduct general overheads, founder drawings, owner distributions, marketing budgets, ordinary staff costs, unrelated software subscriptions, arbitrary administration charges, owner tax or taxes on the Operator's profits.

Non-subscription revenue, including separately commissioned projects, sponsorship, merchandise or one-off sales, is excluded unless added by a written policy accepted before that revenue is earned. The Operator must keep a reasonable record of gross receipts, deductions, VAT treatment, eligible net receipts and the pool calculation.

If a refund or chargeback relating to an earlier period is identified after that period has been closed, the Operator must show the adjustment separately in the next statement. It must not make an undisclosed deduction from a creator's already-paid share. Any recovery of an overpayment must follow C8.

## C3. Calculation and equal shares

For each monthly accounting period, the Creator Pool is 50% of Eligible Net Subscription Revenue unless changed prospectively under C10. The pool for that month, together with any carried-forward unallocated balance, is divided into equal individual shares among all Eligible Creators for that period. A person receives one share only, regardless of the number of approved roles or qualifying works. Multiple roles do not create multiple shares.

If no Eligible Creators qualify for a period, the unallocated pool is carried forward and added to the next month's Creator Pool. It remains identified in the Creator Pool ledger and is not automatically transferred to the Operator, the NightJar operating pool or the owners. If there are still no eligible creators, it carries forward again.

Amounts are calculated in whole pence. Any remainder penny must be allocated using a rotating order: eligible creators are placed in a stable, reproducible order for calculation, the starting position advances from the previous period, and the order continues until all remainder pennies are allocated. A creator must not systematically receive the extra penny because their account identifier sorts first. The allocation record must show the method and starting position used.

## C4. Eligibility

A person is an Eligible Creator for a period only if:
(a) their relevant professional role is approved and active;
(b) they have accepted this Schedule;
(c) they have completed the applicable published commitment requirements for that period;
(d) the qualifying work has passed the stated acceptance or release criteria;
(e) they are not suspended for a substantiated serious breach directly affecting eligibility; and
(f) the Operator has sufficient verified payment details.

The exact number, type and duration of qualifying works, qualification period, cut-off date and evidence requirements must be recorded in a Creator Pool Eligibility Table before the period begins. It must be accessible to contributors and cannot be changed retrospectively.

The table must be role-sensitive and proportionate. Audio duration may be relevant for some roles but cannot be a universal test that unfairly excludes artwork or editing measured differently. Short-form, mini-release or other exceptions qualify only where the table or a written individual exception says so. Exceptions must be recorded consistently. The Operator must not make a Contributor ineligible solely because it delays publication of work that it has already accepted, unless the published eligibility table clearly and fairly makes public release a necessary criterion for that particular role.

A creator eligible through multiple roles still receives one share. A person must not be excluded merely for making a good-faith complaint, requesting lawful removal or exercising a statutory right. If the Operator suspends eligibility, it must give the reason, identify the affected period and provide a review route. Amounts not reasonably in dispute must not be withheld.

## C5. Founder opt-out

The parties record the current policy that **Meg, as a NightJar owner, does not participate in or receive payments from the Creator Pool**. Her exclusion does not create an additional bonus or special share for any particular creator. The full Creator Pool is divided equally among the other Eligible Creators.

If Meg performs creative work, that alone does not make her eligible while the opt-out remains in force. Owner distributions, drawings, salary or other founder compensation are separate and must not be represented as guaranteed or paid from creator entitlements. Their legal and tax treatment depends on the Operator's actual legal structure.

Any future change to the founder opt-out applies prospectively and cannot alter accrued creator entitlements.

## C6. Statements and payment timing

The Operator calculates the pool monthly after subscription receipts and adjustments are reconciled. Within 20 calendar days after month end, each Eligible Creator receives a statement showing the period, Eligible Net Subscription Revenue, pool percentage and total, carry-forward balance, number of Eligible Creators, individual share, any amount held for a documented payment issue and expected payment date.

Undisputed amounts are paid by direct bank transfer no later than 30 calendar days after month end, subject to lawful identity, fraud and payment checks. The intended account is the Operator's Monzo business account, or a replacement business account notified to creators. NightJar does not require a creator to send money to another creator through the platform. Private creator-to-creator transactions remain directly between those creators.

If payment cannot be completed because bank details are incorrect or a lawful check is outstanding, the Operator must notify the Contributor. The amount remains owed and is not forfeited.

## C7. Tax, records and deductions

The Creator Pool share is stated as a gross contractual amount before any deduction that the Operator is legally required to make from that particular payment. If a deduction is legally required, the Operator must explain its basis and amount in the payment statement and provide any required evidence or certificate.

The Contributor is responsible for their own tax reporting only to the extent the law places that responsibility on them. This Schedule does not decide employment status, VAT registration or whether PAYE or National Insurance obligations arise. The Operator must obtain appropriate tax advice and comply with any legal duties that apply.

The Operator's optional business tax reserve is not a tax payment on behalf of the Contributor and must not be deducted from the Creator Pool unless the deduction is expressly agreed and lawful.

## C8. Queries and corrections

A Contributor may query a statement within 60 days of receipt. The Operator must investigate and provide a reasoned response within 20 Working Days where reasonably practicable. Obvious accounting errors may be corrected with a written explanation. An overpayment must not be recovered by arbitrary deduction from unrelated fees; any recovery must be lawful, proportionate and discussed. A genuine dispute over part of a payment does not permit withholding an undisputed amount.

## C9. No guarantee or ownership interest

Eligibility does not guarantee a particular amount. The share depends on actual Eligible Net Subscription Revenue and the number of Eligible Creators in the period. This Schedule does not transfer copyright, create a partnership or joint venture between creators, grant control of NightJar or create a shareholding. A commission fee remains payable under its Statement of Work even if the Contributor is not Creator Pool eligible, unless that Statement of Work clearly and lawfully provides otherwise.

## C10. Changes

Changes to the percentage, eligibility table or administration process apply only to future accounting periods. A material change reducing the percentage, making eligibility materially harder or changing the calculation basis requires at least 30 days' written notice and the Contributor's express acceptance before it applies to that Contributor.

If the Contributor does not accept a material change, they may end participation in the Creator Pool without losing accrued amounts. No change may be backdated or remove an entitlement already earned. An administrative improvement that does not reduce rights or make eligibility harder may be implemented prospectively with notice.
