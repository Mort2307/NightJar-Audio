These Terms supplement Nightjar's Platform Terms for approved VA Creators, Writers, Audio Editors and Artists. They do not replace a separate agreed project brief, licence, commissioning agreement, or individual earnings schedule. They must not promise rates or rights beyond those actually implemented, signed and funded.

## 1. Professional eligibility and role approval

1.1 All professionals must be at least 18 and must complete any verification, identity, tax and rights checks reasonably required for their role, applicable law, the acquiring bank or the content programme. Approval is role-specific. One account may hold multiple professional roles without combining ownership records for different contributions.

1.2 Nightjar may open application waves based on operational capacity, moderation, budget and safeguarding requirements. A pending application is not a job offer. Approval does not promise a minimum number of releases, work offers, subscriptions, listens or payments.

1.3 No professional should begin paid work in reliance on a proposed dashboard rate until the applicable written engagement or compensation schedule is agreed. Employment, worker and self-employment status will depend on the real relationship and law, not solely on a label in these Terms.

## 2. Ownership of submitted work

2.1 The professional keeps ownership of copyright and related rights they legally own in original scripts, recorded performances, editing contributions, original visual art and other submitted work. Upload alone does not assign copyright to Nightjar.

2.2 Ownership of a completed collaborative release is not automatically allocated to a single person. Each contributor must clarify underlying script copyright, performances, sound-recording rights, production/master rights, artwork, music, sound libraries, final approvals, licences and attribution, preferably in an accepted signed project agreement before publication.

2.3 Uploading or crediting another contributor's work does not transfer that contributor's rights. Nightjar may require proof of authority before accepting, publishing or monetising a work.

## 3. Licence to Nightjar for approved published content

3.1 For content deliberately submitted and approved for publication, and only insofar as the professional owns or controls the relevant rights, the professional grants Nightjar a non-exclusive, worldwide, royalty-bearing **only where a separate payment schedule so states**, licence during the agreed publication period to host, back up, process, transcode, reproduce, communicate to the public and stream that content within Nightjar, and to create incidental technical copies needed for security and delivery.

3.2 Limited promotion using the public title, contributor credit, non-sensitive metadata and an agreed promotional preview may take place only within the scope expressly selected during submission or a separate promotional consent. Nightjar will not treat this licence as permission to transfer copyright ownership, sell a full manuscript, distribute raw files to other studios, clone a voice or train a generative model.

3.3 Nightjar may grant narrowly limited technical sublicences to contracted hosting, caching, delivery, payment or content-moderation processors only as necessary to provide the authorised service, subject to appropriate contractual protections. Any wider syndication, exclusivity, audiobook sale, derivative adaptation, merchandise or external distribution requires a separate signed agreement.

3.4 For draft, private manuscript, private showcase or pre-publication files, the permission is limited to secure storage, processing, permissions-based review and the collaboration functions the contributor expressly enables. Such files are not granted a public distribution licence by mere upload.

## 4. Permissions, age and performer consent

4.1 Before submission, each uploader warrants that they have documented any copyright licences, releases and third-party permissions needed for the chosen publication, territory and format. This includes music, samples, scripts, voice performances, artwork, production masters, commissioned edits and identifiable real-person material.

4.2 Actual performers and participants contributing to sexually explicit material must be adults and must have freely and knowingly consented to the particular performance and distribution. Nightjar may require age-and-consent evidence or confirmation of where valid records are securely kept, to the extent required by law, card networks, acquirers or a documented risk assessment. Sensitive identity documents must not be published on profiles.

4.3 Do not include a real person's cloned or imitated voice, intimate personal information or purported consent without genuine permission. Do not falsely claim authorisation, professional accreditation, an external credit or access to another person's manuscript.

4.4 If a right or performer consent is withdrawn or disputed, immediately report it through [RIGHTS CONTACT]. Nightjar may pause dissemination and review the governing agreements and legal position. A consent dispute must not be treated as a routine takedown request where safeguarding or criminal conduct may be implicated.

## 5. Content rules and editorial control

5.1 All submissions must comply with the Platform Terms and any clear published content policy. Nightjar prohibits sexual exploitation of minors or people presented as minors, child-presentation age-play, non-consensual intimate material, sexual exploitation of incapacitated persons, grooming, trafficking, unlawful extreme pornography, threats, stolen content and other illegal material.

5.2 Fictional material is not exempt from applicable law. Nightjar reserves the right to prohibit additional categories and to require card-network/acquirer content controls as part of production approval.

5.3 Submission may be reviewed before publication, and Nightjar may restrict, reject, remove or unpublish content where reasonably necessary for legal compliance, safety, rights protection, brand policy or processor requirements. Reasons and appeals will be offered where legally required. Nightjar does not have to distribute a work merely because the owner has uploaded it.

## 6. Role-specific requirements

6.1 **VA Creators:** confirm performer rights, releases from other voice actors, appropriate music/SFX licences, underlying script licences, master-recording rights, material descriptions and all required performer-age evidence. Content must be fully cleared before public release.

6.2 **Writers:** remain the authors of original manuscripts unless a valid written transfer specifies otherwise. Sharing a protected script with an approved collaborator grants only the specific reading/evaluation access selected, not an automatic adaptation, recording, exclusivity or resale right. Significant edits, production, casting and publication require the permissions specified in the project agreement.

6.3 **Audio Editors:** may showcase only samples they own or are authorised to publish. Client scripts, raw stems, private performer recordings and unreleased material must not be exposed in a portfolio without the rights holder's express permission. Self-reported skills must not be described as platform-verified credentials.

6.4 **Artists:** must identify the rights held in artwork and have permission to use underlying reference material, marks and imagery of identifiable persons. Nightjar's human-created-work requirements apply where stated in the submission flow. Commission work requires a separate specification of permitted uses, credit, revisions and final file delivery.

6.5 **Multi-role professionals:** Nightjar may display one coherent public account with multiple professional sections, but assignments, permissions, licenses, revisions, credits, projects and earnings must remain attributable to the correct role and work.

## 7. Collaboration and individual project agreements

7.1 Before any paid collaboration or public release, record contributor identities (where lawfully required), the scope of the deliverable, deadlines, approval workflow, master and underlying IP ownership, licences, credits, territories, duration, revision limits, payment schedule, cancellation rules and removal conditions.

7.2 No Nightjar profile field, direct message, audition, credit or collaboration invite should be interpreted by itself as an assignment of copyright or an obligation to perform unpaid work.

7.3 A disagreement between collaborators may lead to a publication hold. Nightjar will not pretend to determine disputed legal ownership merely from a platform upload log. We may require documentation or a final legal resolution before restoring distribution.

## 8. Compensation and funding — no automatic £3,000 entitlement

8.1 Professional approval and participation do not alone create a right to a standard payment or a share of gross subscription receipts. Payment terms must be agreed in a separate written engagement or a clearly accepted, published compensation schedule identifying eligible roles, deliverables, rates, cap, accrual, tax treatment, settlement timing and termination.

8.2 Nightjar's current **internal planning model** allocates up to 50% of **Eligible Net Revenue** to eligible creator compensation, with 30% modelled for the operator and 20% for the business fund. These are planning policy assumptions, not unconditional individual entitlements. The model also considers reserves, downturns, unfilled paid slots and a proposed £3,000 monthly cap per eligible paid VA Creator. Nothing in this clause promises that any particular role or person will receive that cap or even a paid slot.

8.3 If incorporated into a future signed payment agreement, **Eligible Net Revenue** must be defined unambiguously using actual settled receipts, relevant VAT/sales taxes, refunds, chargebacks, processor/acquirer fees, currency fees and any other expressly disclosed permitted deductions. The same contract must explain whether reserves are deducted when withheld or only when a loss becomes final, how reserve releases enter later accounting periods, and how revenue, gift balances and contributor accruals are reconciled.

8.4 No individual contribution may be marketed as a guaranteed fixed income or as remuneration determined solely by popularity unless their actual contract makes that promise. Listener engagement may support discovery analytics without automatically determining a professional's base pay.

8.5 If lawful and payment-provider approved, Nightjar may pay eligible professional earnings by documented bank transfer, using verified beneficiary details and accounting records. The specific payout threshold, cadence, dispute route and chargeback clawback arrangements must be agreed in advance. Gift credits and subscription-derived compensation must be accounted for separately.

8.6 Each professional is responsible for appropriate tax declarations and business records except where Nightjar has an obligation to deduct, withhold, report or account for tax under law. Nightjar may require invoices, tax status information and identity verification to administer lawful payments.

## 9. Gifts, wallet liabilities and withheld payments

9.1 A proposed Nightjar gift token is not a bank deposit, savings product or financial investment. It must not be sold or represented as having cash value for purchasers unless legal and payment-provider arrangements explicitly support that model. The actual consumer terms at purchase must describe the permitted use and refund rights.

9.2 A professional's entitlement to a gift-related award arises only under the expressly agreed eligible-pay terms after relevant clearing and fraud checks; no gift receipt shown in beta guarantees bank transfer. Nightjar may hold disputed or suspect transfers for fair investigation, preserving audit trails and required consumer rights.

9.3 A rolling reserve imposed on Nightjar by a processor is a timing restriction on Nightjar's liquidity, not automatically the contributor's financial loss. No deduction from a contributor's earned entitlement is permitted without a clear contractual basis and applicable law.

## 10. Privacy, confidentiality and secure materials

10.1 Contributors must protect confidential manuscripts, drafts, client files, private message details, unapproved artwork, payment information and the personal data of collaborators. Only use access permissions necessary for the agreed work.

10.2 Do not copy private materials to public sites, shared cloud accounts, AI model-training services or unauthorised third parties. Mandatory records may be retained in accordance with the Privacy Notice, statutory requirements and dispute-resolution needs.

10.3 Nightjar's no-generative-AI-training default applies to all protected submissions. Any different permitted use requires a separate clear rights agreement and appropriate data-protection basis; a general platform update will not silently establish such permission.

## 11. Termination, removal and surviving licences

11.1 A professional may stop accepting new collaborations and ask to unpublish their own authorised work using [PROFESSIONAL SUPPORT ROUTE]. The contractual notice period and effect of removing a published collaborative work must follow the applicable signed release/project agreement, including rights owned by others and outstanding lawful consumer obligations.

11.2 Nightjar may suspend professional privileges for safeguarding, serious contract breach, unlawful material, material rights defects, fraud, sanctions or processor requirements, acting proportionately and preserving any obligations to pay accrued sums properly due under a binding agreement.

11.3 End of account access does not automatically void licences previously granted to others in valid standalone agreements, invalidate lawful retained audit evidence or erase accrued payment rights. The parties will cooperate to close works and reconcile records as required.

## 12. Complaints, disputes and changes

12.1 A professional may report payment errors, publishing or rights disputes, safety concerns and moderation decisions to [PROFESSIONAL DISPUTES CONTACT]. Nightjar will offer the applicable reasons, challenge process and legal remedies.

12.2 Material changes to royalties, permitted uses, exclusivity or professional deliverables require the agreement and notice required by the operative contract and applicable law. Earlier rights grants cannot be expanded retroactively merely by posting new website terms.

12.3 These Terms are governed by the law of England and Wales, subject to mandatory law applicable to the parties and engagement. No clause excludes fraud, death or personal injury due to negligence, or other liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

## 13. Signed commercial schedule required before payout

Before paying a professional, record in a separate engagement signed or accepted by both parties: (a) legal identities; (b) role and scope; (c) work ownership and licences; (d) applicable rate and any cap; (e) calculation of net receipts; (f) eligibility/deliverable condition; (g) settlement and reserve policy; (h) chargeback allocation; (i) tax treatment; (j) termination and outstanding-pay treatment; (k) dispute process; and (l) payment and contact information. **Do not treat the financial simulator as this signed schedule.**
